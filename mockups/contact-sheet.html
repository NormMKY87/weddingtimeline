Product mockups, contact sheet

Six products and the bundle, each at four sizes: landscape 1920 by 1080, square 1080, portrait 1080 by 1350, and a transparent hero cutout 1600 by 1200 for the sales page. Every scene is HTML and CSS in mockups/scenes/, rendered with node mockups/render.js. Palette, type and the brand wordmark come from mockups/tokens.css, so the whole suite re-skins from one file once the education brand name lands.
