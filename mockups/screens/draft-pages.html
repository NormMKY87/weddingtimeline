How to use this page

The anniversary note

Send toCouples married one to five years ago
WhenThree weeks before their date
The line to changeOne photograph from the day

Subject: [One year] already

Hi [Names],

[One year] ago this [Saturday] you were [standing at the top of the stairs at the venue], and I still think about that frame.

I am opening a small number of anniversary sessions in [month]. An hour, somewhere that matters to you both, and a gallery inside [two weeks].

I am holding them for couples I have already photographed until [date]. If you would like one, reply to this email and I will send the days I have free.

[Your name]

Why it is written this way

The photograph comes first because it proves the email is for them. The offer is one thing with an end date. The ask is a reply, never a booking link, because a reply costs them nothing.

When to skip it

Skip it if their gallery went out less than six months ago, or if you have nothing open in the month you name.

When to skip it

Skip it if the couple has asked for their photographs to stay private. Ask them first, every time.

How the playbook is sorted

Three lists, worked in this order

Start with the people who already know you. Then the people who send you work. Strangers come last, because they take the longest to say yes.

The listWho is on itCan pay back in
1People who know youPast couples, their families, enquiries from the last twelve monthsDays
2People who send you workVenues, planners, vendors, other photographersWeeks
3People who have never heard of youSearch, social, directories, pressMonths

How to use it

Pick one play from the first list today and run it before you read the rest. Every play fits on one page and lists the time it takes, what you need and when to skip it.

What a play is

One action you can finish in a sitting. It ends with a message sent or a to-do set, never with something to think about.

Keeping score

Write the date beside each play you run, and the date of the first enquiry it brings in. After three months you will know which five plays are yours.

Play 04  /  People who send you work

The venue gallery

Time it takesForty five minutes
Can pay back inTwo to six weeks
You needOne recent wedding at the venue

Planners and venues refer the people who make their job easier. Photographs they can use this week do that.

  1. Pick the last wedding you photographed at a venue you want more of.
  2. Choose twenty frames that show the venue well. Rooms, light, details, and one or two of the couple.
  3. Check with the couple that they are happy for the venue to use them.
  4. Export at web size and put them in a gallery the coordinator can download from.
  5. Send the venue gallery email with one specific thank you in the second line.
  6. Set a to-do for two weeks from today to see where the photographs were used.

When to skip it

Skip it if your last wedding there was more than a year ago. Photograph there again first, then send.

Play 05  /  People who send you work

The open dates swap

Time it takesThirty minutes
Can pay back inOne to eight weeks
You needThree photographers you trust

Every photographer turns down dates they are already booked for. Those couples still need someone, and they will take a name from the person they wanted.

  1. List three photographers whose work sits near yours and whose couples would like you.
  2. Send each one your open dates for the next six months.
  3. Ask for theirs. Keep both lists somewhere you can find in ten seconds.
  4. When an enquiry lands on a date you cannot do, reply with a name inside the hour.
  5. Tell the other photographer you sent them, so they know to look for it.

When to skip it

Skip it if you would not hand your own sibling's wedding to them. Your name travels with the referral.
