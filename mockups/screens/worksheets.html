The Seven Day Checklist

Six quick wins, one a day, then a day to measure. None of them needs a wedding to happen first. Every one shows a change you can see inside two weeks.

  • 1
    Reply within the hour and book the callBookings are lost to silence. Set the alert, write the one line reply, keep it in your phone.
    Done
  • 2
    Set a two day follow-up to-do on every leadIn the CRM or the calendar, the moment you reply.
    Done
  • 3
    Add one priced add-on this monthSecond shooter, album or travel. Average sale rises without touching the base price.
    Done
  • 4
    Record your next consult and listen backWith consent. The fastest skill jump available.
    Done
  • 5
    Check the date across every calendar before confirmingPersonal, business and roster. Never take back a yes.
    Done
  • 6
    Outsource one cull or edit batch as a testOne wedding, one invoice. Note the hours it gave back.
    Done
  • 7
    MeasureReply time, follow-ups set, add-on sold, hours returned. Write the numbers here.

Your number

Run the Quit-Number Calculator with your real payslip, then write the result here so it is on paper, in your handwriting, where you will see it.

The quit number, business profit a year before tax
The gap a month
Provisional quit date
Bookings needed at today's average
Bookings needed at a higher average
Cash buffer, months of household expenses
Weddings booked this year

Compare net to net. The job pays you after tax, super and leave. The business has to clear all three before the numbers match. If the gap is negative, the question is the buffer and the calendar, not the maths.

Next: Lesson 1.2, build the package ladder backwards from this number. Twenty weddings at a higher average beats forty at a lower one.
