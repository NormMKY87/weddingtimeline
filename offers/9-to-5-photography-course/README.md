# The Shift Change: offer design

Single source of truth: the-shift-change-full-offer-design.md

A complete Grand Slam Offer design for the career transition product aimed at shift workers who want a dated plan to step off the roster into paid photography. Built and stress tested with the $100M Offers method. Contains the offer on one page, the book's method run step by step against the idea, a 70 problem list, solutions, delivery vehicles, trim and stack, the final stack with values, enhancers, pricing ladder, a 38 objection map, the pre-sell plan and the Offer Canvas.
