# Wedding 5 Proposal Follow-up: Six Emails for Norm's Review

Status: draft copy. Not loaded into any workflow, not sent.

Conventions, matched to the existing W01 to W06 Enquiry templates:

- Greeting uses {{contact.first_name}} only. "You both" replaces the partner merge field so nothing renders blank for Instagram leads without a partner name on file.
- No typed sign-off. The existing templates carry none, which indicates the hello@ signature is appended at send time. If the signature check in the build spec (section 4.8) finds no signature, add a single line "Norman" to each email.
- Every proposal link is {{opportunity.dubsado_proposal_link}}. The guard before each email refuses to send if that field is empty or does not contain https://, so no email can go out with a blank or wrong link.
- No availability claims, no scarcity, no expiry dates, no discounts, no testimonials, no suggestion that the date has been released or the enquiry closed.
- Tone matched to W04 and W05: warm, unhurried, one clear ask, permission to say no.
- Day 14 answers a question using only facts already in the account: the wedding timeline planner is the tool Norm uses with every couple to map the day (from W04).

Link anchor text is shown in square brackets; in the builder, hyperlink that text to {{opportunity.dubsado_proposal_link}}.

## Email 4, day 14: A useful question answered

Subject: How much coverage do you actually need?

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

One question I'm asked more than any other at this stage is how many hours of photography a wedding really needs. The honest answer is that it depends on the shape of your day, not on a rule of thumb.

The way I work it out with every couple is to map the day from getting ready through to the last dance, then see where the photography naturally starts and finishes. Travel time between locations, how long portraits take with your group, and whether you want the send-off covered all shift the number more than people expect.

If you'd like, send me a rough outline of your day and I'll tell you honestly whether the coverage in your proposal is right, too much, or too little. You can open the proposal here: [view your proposal].
