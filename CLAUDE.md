# Part-Time to Full-Time: Photographer Education

Claude Code project for Norm's photographer education business: digital products and a 90 day flagship program for part-time wedding photographers with a day job who want to go full time. Runs under Norman Yap the educator, on a separate channel, list and name from Norman Yap Photography (NYP).

## Start every session here

1. Read `context/part-time-to-full-time-context.md`. It is the single source of context. Do not contradict it. If a decision changes, update that file in the same commit.
2. Read `plan/build-order.md` for what is due now.
3. Check the Asana board before creating or moving work. GIDs are in `context/asana-board.md`. The board is the task tracker, this repo holds the written output.

## PC versus cloud sessions

The NYP source material (enquiry workflow, ESE proposal template, DM scripts, KPI tracker, Dubsado setup, contracts, vendor emails) lives in Norm's Claude memory and skills on the PC. Writing that pulls from it happens in PC sessions. Cloud sessions handle planning, structure, the Asana board, and anything that needs no NYP source material.

## Rules that do not move

- Test demand before delivery. The Booking Pack sells before anything is filmed.
- One outcome per program. Nine actions or fewer. One insight and one tool per action.
- Two to four hours of the student's week. Extend the program or cut content, never cram.
- Quick wins first. Week zero actions must show measurable change inside two weeks.
- Bonuses stay locked until core actions are done.
- Innovate live, then automate. Teach once to the founding intake, film once.
- Separate channel, list and name from NYP. Couple-facing NYP content and educator content never cross.
- No prices anywhere in this project until Vance (Norm's finance bot) sets them.
- Anything not on the side products list waits until the Booking Pack has sold 20 copies.
- Low-ticket buyers get credited or upgraded into the flagship. Never sell the same scripts twice.

## Writing and voice (all product copy, lessons, emails, sales pages)

- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, full stops, parentheses.
- No semicolons. No "not just X, but also Y". No "it's not X, it's Y".
- Short sentences, active voice, specific nouns and numbers, varied sentence length.
- First person singular in anything from Norm: "I" and "my", never "we" or "our studio".
- No generic headings (Key Takeaways, Why It Matters, Final Thoughts). No rhetorical questions as transitions. No dramatic one-line fragments.
- Sound like a person with a point of view, not a summary engine.
- Client-facing copy is warm, human, conversational. Never templated.
- Australian English spelling.
