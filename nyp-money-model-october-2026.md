# The NYP Money Model, One Offer Per Slot

Prepared 14 October 2026. Built on the Money Model doctrine in Vance OS (MM and LC page cites as used there), the 3 September offer review, and the locked growth plays. Internal. The confirmed collections are not yet released and nothing here goes to a couple.

## Where the money model actually stands

A Money Model is a deliberate sequence of offers: attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity (MM p.26, p.172). The first test is Customer Financed Acquisition: thirty day gross profit above twice the cost of acquiring the customer (LC p.47).

NYP passes that test on every rung before doing anything, because a 50 percent retainer on a five figure ticket arrives twelve to eighteen months before any direct cost. Thirty day cash on the confirmed ladder, ex GST, derived here from price divided by 1.1 and halved:

| Collection | Price inc GST | 30 day cash | Covers 2x CAC plus direct cost (September lines) |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Ceremony | $6,400 | $2,909 | $1,886, clears |
| The Story | $9,500 | $4,318 | $2,889, clears |
| The Feature | $13,500 | $6,136 | $3,169, clears |
| The Heirloom | $16,950 | $7,705 | $4,869, clears |

So the Money Model's job at NYP is not cash timing. It is the other two levers (LC p.48): lower CAC and raise LTGP, inside a hard cap. The cap is Norm's hours: about 613 used at the 26 wedding shape, about 1,387 left for selling, admin, marketing and every offer below. Every offer is therefore scored on gross profit per Norm hour added, which is the complexity brake (LC p.80).

On the five step ladder (MM p.172) NYP is at step one, customers arriving reliably, and that step is unmeasured. The book's rule for that position is one offer at a time, perfected, measured in quarters (MM p.176). The sequence at the end respects that.

## A1. The Feature Giveaway (Attraction: Giveaway, with Buy X Get Y Free as the second prize)

**Source.** Transcript 22, the Boulder money model, flagged by Norm as the most important for NYP. The attraction offer sits in front of the ladder and its job is cost per lead, not revenue.

**The offer.** One 2027 or 2028 couple has The Feature on us. Entry is one short form with no gates. Selection on their story, as Balmain runs it. Ten day window, countdown, referral multiplier on the thank you page, daily count down emails and texts.

**Second prize, which is where the money is.** Every other entrant is told on the close date that they are a second prize winner. More than one couple can win it, never everyone. The prize is Buy X Get Y Free with a 72 hour claim window: book The Story and the fine art album comes with it; book The Feature and the two parent albums come with it. The bonus costs album COGS only when thirty day cash has landed, so it is acquisition cost, not gift budget.

**The money line.**

| Line | Figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Prize cost | About $1,900 direct plus 20.7 Norm hours, roughly $4,200 all in | September cost lines and Norm's wage build up |
| 30 day cash per second prize Story booking | $4,318 | Confirmed ladder, 50 percent retainer, ex GST |
| Allowable spend per booking | $618 working, $1,236 at the 2x rule | The review's figures; C020 is contested, so working not floor |
| Boulder result | 3,700 leads, best cost per lead ever, 325 percent on the offer sold | Haircut by five for Perth before forecasting anything |
| Break even | One Story booking on 30 day cash; two with ads loaded | Derived |

**Governors.** Norm is the whole sales team, so the claim is self serve: the 72 hour button books a consult, Evelyn handles replies, nobody is called until a consult is held. Expect a third to half of entries in the last 48 hours. Every entrant is stamped with source and wedding date, which is the measurement the review said the business lacks.

**Gate.** The review blocks any new lead pond until the source stamp has ninety days of baseline, which lands in early December. Build in November, launch mid January 2027 into proposal season, in front of the 2028 opening.

## A2. The Monthly Intake, completed (Attraction: Giveaway, continuous)

**Source.** Case 12 transplant: the 72 hour second prize claim is the piece NYP has never had, and Norm confirmed the non winners were never reminded. Engagement offer rebuild, August.

**The fix, three parts.**

1. Second prize for every applicant not selected: book The Story inside 72 hours of the result and the Editorial Engagement Session is added to it. That costs 4.5 of Norm's hours only after $4,318 of thirty day cash has arrived, and it places the referral ask (end of the engagement session) in front of Story couples too.
2. The 72 hour window, with the countdown in the result email.
3. The Trial With Penalty governor from D1 on every session awarded.

**Money line.** A gifted session is 4.5 Norm hours, about $495 at his wage build up. The cap stays as ruled: one weekday block a month, none in November, December, February or March, eight blocks a year, one governed pool for all certificates.

**Gate.** None. This is a repair to a live offer, not a new pond, so it can ship in December.

## A3. The 2028 Opening Bonus (Attraction: Buy X Get Y Free, time bound by a real reason)

**Source.** MM on stating the reason why (early bird, seasonal, closing), Lesson 103 (the grandfathered price rise, C048), Case 04 (bonuses over discounts, each worth the whole price).

**The offer.** The 2028 calendar opens on a stated date with a declared number of willing dates. The first ten dates booked at The Feature carry the two parent albums; at The Story, the fine art album. Stated in advance, in kind, never a dollar off the headline. Paired with the grandfathered price rise letter to every open proposal: the collections change on this date, anything signed before it is at the current collections.

**Gate.** Respects the hold rules (no price moves until 20 consults have run, Story floor, the language system ships with the ladder). The webinar review's caution stands: a deadline on a joint decision pushes one partner ahead of the other, so the bonus window is weeks, not days, and the reason is said out loud.

## U1. Albums in the First 48 Hours (Upsell: Classic)

**Source.** C029 the first 48 hours (shortlist item 5), Case 09 (sell mid flight, mid decision asks take half to two thirds against a third at the end), Case 04 (sell before the value is delivered), the offer architecture note that parents are the one group with willingness to pay and no product aimed at them.

**The offer.** Inside 48 hours of the retainer: a personal note restating their why, one real quick win (their venue's timeline template), the next milestone booked, and the adjacent basket offered once at the peak: the fine art album and the two parent albums, which is the Feature to Heirloom step of $3,450. Re asked at two later peaks only, the 48 hour preview and the gallery reveal. The album design consult is booked in the room (BAM FAM).

**Money line.** Taken at booking, half the add on lands inside the thirty day window, so this is the one offer that lifts the prized metric directly. Zero Saturdays, design hours only. Hormozi's band for true new sale upsells is 20 to 40 percent; use it as a band, not a forecast.

**Gate.** Album and parent album COGS must land first; C050 has The Heirloom under the 80 percent gross margin floor on its album lines. Price the basket after COGS, then ship as the first material change in December.

## U2. The Session Rollover (Upsell: Rollover)

**Source.** Case 07 transplant, named verbatim: rollover credit as NYP's paid front end. LC on paid front ends showing up (85 to 90 percent). MM Rollover Upsell: credit prior spend toward the next offer.

**The offer.** Couples who buy a standalone engagement session ($1,600) or a pre wedding session ($1,800 to $3,500) are a pond that currently dead ends. Rule stated at purchase: your session fee rolls into product credit (album or wall art) on any collection booked within 30 days of your session gallery. The collection headline price stays whole, which keeps the gifts in kind rule intact and keeps the session a trust ladder, not a discount.

**Money line.** Thirty day cash on the session is the full fee, about $1,455 ex GST on $1,600, collected before any wedding is sold. The credit costs album COGS only on a booked collection. Session hours are already being spent today; nothing is added.

**Gate.** Not a price change, not a payment schedule change, not a guarantee, not a new pond. Vance confirms the credit mechanic against the gifts in kind rule, then it ships with the April to September session window.

## D1. The Session Governor (Downsell: Trial With Penalty)

**Source.** MM Trial With Penalty: free if they meet the terms, they pay if they do not. LC on paid commitments and show rates. Vance's open flag on every gifted session consuming Norm's hours.

**The offer.** Every gifted or certificate engagement session is free on two terms: attend the booked weekday block and complete the prep questionnaire. A card is held at booking; a stated fee applies only on a no show or a cancellation inside seven days. This is the governor the growth plays asked for before either certificate mechanic launches.

**Money line.** Protects 4.5 Norm hours per session, 54 gifted hours a year at the cap. Expected revenue nil. Its value is the hours it stops leaking and the show rate it buys.

**Gate.** Norm's no deposit ruling is on consults. This is sessions, and it is a hold not a payment. Confirm the distinction with him before it ships with A2.

## The standing rule

Each offer enters the Monday pulse as a falsifiable forecast with its metric and a review date. At fifty consults the close rate confidence interval is plus or minus fourteen points, so no offer here is judged on close rate. They are judged on cost per booked wedding by source, thirty day cash per booking, and gross profit per Norm hour.
