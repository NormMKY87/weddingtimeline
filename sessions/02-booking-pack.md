2. Enquiry-to-Booking sequence. Staged first reply whose only job is to book the call, the three-calendar date check, the competing enquiry paragraph, the two day follow-up to-do. Adapt `01-journey-map.md` and `02-email-templates.md`.

4. Objection replies. Price pushback, the budget question, still deciding, partner wants someone else, fewer hours. Norm's position on discounting (never discount, never gift the album, credits not discounts).

## Rules

- Rewritten for a stranger. Strip venue names, client names, NYP prices and anything that only makes sense inside NYP. If a line needs NYP context, cut or rewrite it.
- Buyers are credited or upgraded into the flagship. These scripts are never sold twice. The Module 2 and 3 worksheets adapt this material, they do not duplicate it.
- No prices in the product. The checkout price is Vance's call.
- Voice rules in `CLAUDE.md`. First person singular. Australian English. Warm, human, never templated in tone even though it is a template.
