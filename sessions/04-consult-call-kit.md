## What Norm asked for (8 Nov 2026)

Assemble the tools NYP uses to get the best outcome from a consult, rewritten for a stranger:

1. The call run sheet. Opening, discovery, the wedding day walk-through, next steps, close.
2. The timeline builder as a consult tool. Walking the couple through their day on the call is both the discovery method and the moment trust is built. Include a short version for the call and point to the full pre-wedding timeline process.
3. The recording habit. Record every consult with consent. Listen back once. Use the transcript to write the proposal and to mine content (the questions couples ask become posts).
4. Objection handling. The five objections and the replies, the CLOSER-W framework as Norm runs it, the position on discounting. Depth here, the Booking Pack carries the short replies.
5. The ten questions that surface budget and decision makers without asking for them.
