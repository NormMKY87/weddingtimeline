# Session 6: The Warm List Emails and The Quiet Month Playbook

Two proposed products, added by Norm on 13 November 2026. Nested under the Digital Products Build group with sessions 1 to 5 (session tag `digital-products-build`). Briefs in `products/warm-list-emails/BRIEF.md` and `products/quiet-month-playbook/BRIEF.md`.

## What Norm asked for (13 Nov 2026)

Two more products in the mockup suite, started from two bonus PDFs in his KT Merry Insiders course folder: an email template set and a list of ways to get more clients. His instruction was to restyle them under his own name and keep the wording close.

## What was built instead, and why

The two source PDFs are another educator's paid course material. Selling a restyled, lightly reworded copy would infringe her copyright and almost certainly breach the course terms, and the buyers are the same small market she sells to. The project already carries this rule for Laura Higgins and Alex Hormozi material.

So this session built the part that needs no source text, and set up the rest to be written from Norm's own practice:

- Both products added to the mockup suite as 08 and 09 under working titles: photo cover, magazine edition, print assets, placeholder inside pages and all twelve photoreal scenes.
- A brief for each product that fixes the source rule and lists what is needed from Norm.
- The PDFs were not opened. The placeholder pages were written from scratch.

## Norm's rulings (14 Nov 2026)

- Both products go on the side products list now. The 20 copies gate is waived for these two.
- The Quiet Month Playbook is a bonus and sits beside the free "25 Ways" series for now.
- Design the material first: content, packaging and design together.
- Norm also asked again for the KT Merry material to be used with wording changes in his voice, keeping its intent, structure and formatting. Not done, for the reason above. Keeping another author's structure and selection with the words changed is still a copy of her work.

## What was built on 14 Nov

- Both products written from scratch, in the voice and page system of the Follow-Up Vault. The KT Merry PDFs were still not opened.
- The Warm List Emails: eleven emails, a list builder, a map and a two week sending plan. 20 pages.
- The Quiet Month Playbook: twenty one plays in three lists, a front door check and a scorecard. 29 pages.
- A shared PDF kit in `products/pdf-kit/` that builds either PDF from its markdown, with the photo cover and full bleed section pages.
- The mockups now show real pages from both PDFs. The placeholder pages are gone.
- A review log in each product folder listing what Norm must confirm, change or cut.

## The unbranded Word file (14 Nov, after draft v0.1 was committed)

Norm then supplied `25_Ways_To_Get_More_Clients_Unbranded.docx` to rework the Playbook from. It was opened to identify it. It is the KT Merry "25 Ways To Get More Clients Right Now" text with the branding removed: same title, her first person voice, her stories. The file properties show it was produced by a conversion script, so it is a copy of the PDF text and carries no licence of its own. Removing the branding does not change whose work it is. Not used. Nothing in either product was changed after it was opened, and the file is not in this repo.

What would change this: written resale or white label rights from KT Merry. If Norm has them, the terms go in `context/` and the rule is revisited.
