## What was built instead, and why

The two source PDFs are another educator's paid course material. Selling a restyled, lightly reworded copy would infringe her copyright and almost certainly breach the course terms, and the buyers are the same small market she sells to. The project already carries this rule for Laura Higgins and Alex Hormozi material.

So this session built the part that needs no source text, and set up the rest to be written from Norm's own practice:

- Both products added to the mockup suite as 08 and 09 under working titles: photo cover, magazine edition, print assets, placeholder inside pages and all twelve photoreal scenes.
- A brief for each product that fixes the source rule and lists what is needed from Norm.
- The PDFs were not opened. The placeholder pages were written from scratch.
