# Session 3: The Follow-Up Vault

Write the Follow-Up Vault, the order bump at Booking Pack checkout. Due 7 December 2026. Asana task 1220327478129133. Brief in `products/follow-up-vault/BRIEF.md`.

## What Norm asked for (8 Nov 2026)

- Six to seven follow-up messages per track as the starting point, and Norm intends to increase the count.
- The first message is always fully personalised. The second is half personalised. From the third on, the message is a template with one variable to change.
- Two tracks: new enquiry (no reply after the first response) and proposal sent (no decision after the proposal).
- Every message by day and channel. Ghosted lead revival closes each track.

## Structure

Track A: new enquiry, no reply
- Day 0: first reply (from the Booking Pack, referenced not repeated)
- Day 2: fully personalised follow-up (one detail from their enquiry, one question)
- Day 5: half personalised (their date or venue plus a template body)
- Day 9: value touch (a useful resource, no ask)
- Day 14: the date check nudge (are you still deciding on this date)
- Day 21: the soft close
- Day 35: ghosted lead revival
- Day 60: polite close-the-file message

Track B: proposal sent, no decision
- Day 0: proposal (from the Booking Pack, referenced not repeated)
- Day 2: fully personalised check-in (one line from the consult transcript)
- Day 5: half personalised (answer the unasked question, usually price or availability)
- Day 8: the competing enquiry paragraph if true, never invented
- Day 12: value touch
- Day 18: the hold or release message
- Day 30: ghosted lead revival
- Day 45: close the file, door open

Each message in email form, with SMS, Instagram DM and WhatsApp variants where the channel fits. Variables marked in square brackets. One line under each message on when to skip it.
