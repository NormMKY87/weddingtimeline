## Conventions

- Subtasks hold the steps. Top-level tasks are the deliverables and milestones.
- Dependencies are set where one deliverable gates another (Booking Pack on sale waits on the Pack, the Vault and pricing, the founding intake waits on the Pack being on sale).
- Finished work: complete the task, comment with the repo path.
