# Five Campaign and Offer Ideas for Norman Yap Photography

Prepared 14 October 2026. Each idea is lifted from a specific Hormozi consult transcript or lesson in the Vance OS library, then corrected for how NYP actually runs. Internal document. The new collections are not yet released, so none of the figures below go to a couple.

## The lens

The 3 September Hormozi offer review ruled that the offer is sound and that nothing on price, tiers, guarantees or payment schedules survived adversarial review. So none of these ideas touch the ladder. Capacity is Norm's willing Saturdays and Norm's own selling hours, so each idea does one of three things: brings dated, engaged couples in at near zero cost, converts couples already held, or makes money without a Saturday. Every idea is tagged at entry with source and wedding date so bookings, not enquiries, get measured. Measurement was the constraint the review named.

Recommended order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Idea 1 first, because Norm flagged its source transcript as the most important for NYP and the economics question is already open with Vance.

## 1. The Feature Giveaway

The Boulder money model, run for one full wedding.

**Source.** Transcript 22 (Paul Fontinelli, Boulder Adventure Park), flagged by Norm as the most important transcript for NYP. Lesson 144 (believability makes free offers convert). The Balmain Estate Day Giveaway, built in September and live now, is the same mechanic and serves as the rehearsal.

**The offer.** One 2027 or 2028 couple has The Feature photographed on us. Every other couple who enters is told after the selection that they are a second prize winner. Second prize is a product bonus with any collection booked inside a 72 hour claim window: the fine art album on The Story, or the two parent albums on The Feature. Never a discount. The bonus only costs anything when a collection is booked, so it is acquisition cost, not gift budget (the Case 10 mechanic).

**Mechanic, in order.**

1. Ten day window, close date on the page, countdown at the top.
2. One short form: names, email, mobile, wedding date, venue or suburb, a story in about 200 words. No gates before contact capture.
3. Thank you page carries a shareable link. Every friend who enters through it earns the referrer another chance, and the referrer of the winner wins too.
4. Emails and texts count the days left.
5. Selection on the story, not a draw, as Balmain runs it. Check the Western Australia trade promotion rules with the adviser before it goes live. Hormozi said this twice.
6. Announce the winner on the close date. The same day, every other entrant receives the second prize with the 72 hour window.
7. After 72 hours, the standard consult invitation goes to the whole list.
8. Then the page flips to the evergreen date check (idea 2).

**NYP corrections.** Say the word free. State the reason why in the first line (launching the new collections, or opening the 2028 calendar). Say that more than one couple can win second prize, never that everyone wins. Put the VSL under the headline and cut the text it replaces. Reviews beside the button, not in a separate band. Because Norm is the entire sales team, the claim is self serve: the 72 hour button books a consult, Evelyn handles the replies, and no human calls anyone until a consult is held. Paul had three salespeople and drowned in 3,700 leads; that is the warning to design around. Expect a third to a half of entries in the last 48 hours, so the middle of the window will look like it is failing. Budget the ads on that curve.

**Fix the Monthly Intake at the same time.** The engagement session non winners have never been reminded. The 72 hour second prize is the piece that has been missing from that offer since August.

**Economics.**

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Cost of the prize | One Feature: about 18 to 24 of Norm's hours, roughly $1,900 direct |
| Boulder result | 3,700 leads in one local market, best cost per lead ever recorded |
| Perth haircut | Divide by five and it is still the largest list of dated, engaged couples NYP has held |
| Break even | Two bookings at The Feature |

**Before launch.** Vance prices the prize and the second prize bonus against the collections P&L. Dubsado form, workflow and canned email cloned from the Balmain build. Evelyn scripts for the claim window. Source tag and wedding date on every entrant.

## 2. Is Your Date Still Open?

The always on raised hand.

**Source.** The engaged lead doctrine in $100M Leads. The nine word email, adapted: are you still looking for your wedding photographer. Move 03 in the Hormozi Applied webinar review, which has never been built as a standalone campaign.

**The offer.** Send us your date and we will tell you if it is open. No call, no pitch, no budget question. One page, one form, two radio buttons that honour whether they want to hear from us again. Reply usually within a couple of days, then beat it.

**Why it fits NYP.** The only honest urgency this market accepts is that the date itself decays. This campaign makes that visible without announcing anything. Some couples will be told the date is gone, and that is the scarcity proof. It captures the one field that matters most, the wedding date, which also settles the couples versus Saturdays question with real data by month.

**Mechanic.**

- Meta and Instagram creative that says only: check your date.
- Open date reply: confirm it, attach the single venue guide if their venue is one of the six, one line on the next step.
- Gone date reply, scripted: confirm it, name two photographers Norm would genuinely send them to, give the venue guide anyway, and ask only whether they want to know if it frees up.
- Runs as the evergreen landing after the giveaway closes, in the footer of every nurture email, and as the fourth path on the Kiera webinar.

**Economics.** Near zero. Judge it on dates captured per month and the share that become a held consult.

## 3. Same Couple, Two Days

The proof campaign for The Feature.

**Source.** Lesson 79 (sales is conviction transfer, fed by fresh proof). Case 10 (the proof loop content format). The 16 September messaging sweep, where five independent spines each named this as the single highest value unpublished asset in the business.

**The campaign.** Six couples, each shown with their engagement session frames beside their wedding day frames, stiff opening frames left in. One reel, one carousel and one plain text email per couple, released fortnightly over three months. One claim in every piece: the odds change when we have already worked together, which is what the Editorial Engagement Session inside The Feature is for.

**Why it is the strongest creative NYP can make.** It lifts the hardest lever in the value equation, perceived likelihood of achievement, which a couple cannot judge from a portfolio. It sells the collection that all three of the last bookings should have been.

**Corrections.** Consent is step zero: in writing, show them the cut, permanent veto. No hour counts, no delivery timing, no image counts. Previews at 48 hours is the only number allowed in copy. The winning pieces get a five second call to action and become the paid creative, so the organic run is the ad test.

## 4. The 2028 Calendar Opening

The launch for the new collections.

**Source.** Lesson 103 (the price rise letter Hormozi actually sent). Lesson 115 (cut supply further than feels comfortable, then price the scarcity). Case 10 (top collection first, referrers paid in status). Case 04 (sell before the value is delivered).

**The campaign.** Declare the willing Saturdays for 2028 as a fixed number and open them in three waves, each with a stated reason.

| Wave | Who | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| One | Past couples and the friends they refer | Two week head start, carried by the two seat certificates from the gallery delivery loop |
| Two | Partners: planners, venues, makeup artists, gown stores | Priority access they can hand to their own couples, which pays them in status rather than money |
| Three | Public | The Feature Giveaway (idea 1) as the attraction in front of it |

**The second half.** The price rise letter to every live enquiry and open proposal: the collections change on a date, here is the date, anything signed before it is at the current collections. That is honest urgency because the change is real, and it is the one deadline a couple deciding jointly will accept.

**Guard rails.** This is a launch, not a price test, so it respects the hold rules: no price moves until 20 consults have run on the new numbers, The Story floor stays, and the ladder ships with the language system or not at all. Timing is Norm's. Top collection first in every consult, step down only on a stated budget.

## 5. The Heirloom Window

Money without a Saturday.

**Source.** Lessons 122 and 142 (sequential upsells on customers already held). Case 09 (sell mid flight at the emotional peak, never months after delivery). Case 05 (the delivered gallery is the in the box moment). The offer architecture note that parents are the one group at a wedding with real willingness to pay and no product aimed at them.

**The campaign.** A fixed window for the couples already on the book, the 26 booked for 2027 at old prices plus every 2026 couple at gallery delivery, to add the fine art album, the two parent albums, and the Portrait Experience where their collection does not already carry it. Three touches, each at a peak: the final timeline meeting, the 48 hour preview, and the gallery reveal. Each touch carries a product bonus for deciding inside the window, never a discount. The album design consult is booked in the room (BAM FAM).

**Why it fits.** The binding constraint is Saturdays and Norm's hours, and albums consume neither. It raises average purchase value on couples with zero acquisition cost, and it reaches the parents through the couple rather than cold.

**Blocker to clear first.** Album and parent album COGS are still unknown, and The Heirloom sits under the 80 percent gross margin floor. Price the window after COGS lands, not before.

## Left on the shelf on purpose

- Consult deposits, an associate tier, a three collection ladder, portrait days as a product. All ruled out by Norm in September.
- Discounts in any wrapper, money back guarantees, dollar values on free tools, capacity bars, bonus ladders after a no, cold outreach, published customer requirements. The Hormozi mechanics the webinar review found do not survive contact with a luxury couple deciding jointly.
- Another lead magnet. Three exist, and the review said the funnel, not the magnet, is the gap.

## What every idea needs before it runs

- The follow up designed before the front end is switched on. Norm is the whole sales team.
- A source tag and a wedding date on every lead, so the result is judged on bookings.
- Evelyn and Dubsado wiring done first, then creative.
