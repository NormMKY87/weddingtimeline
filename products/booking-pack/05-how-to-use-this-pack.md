# Part 5. How to use this pack

One page. The order things fire in, and what to change for your own business.

## The one rule

The first reply's only job is to book the call. Everything in this pack points at that. If you remember nothing else, remember that you are not trying to explain your photography in a DM or an email. You are trying to get 15 minutes.

## The order things fire in

**A couple messages you on Instagram**

1. Check all three calendars if they gave a date.
2. First reply from Part 1. One detail mirrored, one question or the invite. No price, no link.
3. They say yes. Two times, then the booking link, then email and mobile.
4. They go quiet. One nudge inside 24 hours. After that, the Follow-Up Vault.
5. Log the lead by stage.

**A couple fills in your enquiry form**

1. The instant text goes out on its own.
2. Check all three calendars.
3. First reply from Part 2. Date first, one personal line, one question, the call link.
4. Set the two day to-do before you do anything else.
5. They book. The confirmation goes out on its own.
6. They go quiet. The Follow-Up Vault from day two.

**After the call**

1. The proposal email from Part 3, the same day, off your notes from the call.
2. If another couple wants the date, the competing enquiry paragraph goes under the investment line.
3. An objection comes back. Part 4.
4. They go quiet. The Follow-Up Vault, proposal track.
5. Contract signed and retainer paid. The welcome email. Only then is the date booked.

## How to make a script sound like you

1. Read it out loud. Anywhere you stumble is a line you would not say. Change it.
2. Swap my words for yours in the "words I have swapped" table. Keep the shape, lose the phrasing.
3. Write one real sentence for every PERSONAL line, every time. That sentence is the product. The template is the packaging.
4. Never send the same follow-up wording twice to the same couple.
5. After a month, keep the lines that got replies and cut the ones that did not. Your version will be better than mine, because it is yours.

## What this pack does not do

It does not write your follow-ups after a couple goes quiet. That is the Follow-Up Vault, by day and channel. It does not run your call. That is the Consult Call Kit. It does not set your prices. Nothing here tells you what to charge, and nothing should.

If you bought this pack and later join the full program, you will not pay for these scripts twice. They are the foundation of two of its modules, and buyers of this pack are credited or upgraded, never charged again for the same material.
