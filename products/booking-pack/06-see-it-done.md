# Part 6. See it done

My own proposal, page by page, and two recordings of me walking a couple through one. The scripts tell you what to say. This part shows you what it looks like when it happens.

## What my proposal looks like

These are screenshots of the proposal I send to real couples, taken from the page they see. Names, venue and prices are blurred. The numbers are mine to set and yours are yours. What to look at is the shape, the order and the tone.

Everything here is built once as a template. For each couple I change the hero, the letter and anything I tailored on the call. The rest stays.

## Two recordings

Reading a proposal and walking someone through one are different skills. These two recordings are me doing the second one, start to finish, with nothing cut except the figures.

### Recording 1. The 15 minute chat

Audio only, about 15 minutes. A couple has had the proposal for two days and booked a quick call with questions. I open it on my screen, they open it on theirs, and we go through it together. Listen for how little I talk, where I let the silence sit, and how the price is said once and then left alone.

Listen here: [Recording 1 link]

### Recording 2. The hour consultation

A Google Meet recording, about an hour. This is the longer version, for a couple who wants to go through everything before they decide. I share my screen and walk the page top to bottom, in the order they think: do we like you, do we trust how you work, what does it cost, what is the catch, what do we do now. Watch for how each block on the page becomes a short conversation rather than a presentation, and how the next steps are agreed out loud before the call ends.

Watch here: [Recording 2 link]

### How to use them

1. Listen to the 15 minute chat first. It is the one you will do most often.
2. Watch the hour consultation with your own proposal open beside it. Pause at each block and ask whether yours does the same job.
3. Then record yourself doing one. A role play with a friend is fine. Hearing yourself say your price out loud, calmly, once, is most of the work.

The call itself, the questions I ask and the order I ask them in, belongs to the Consult Call Kit. These recordings are about the proposal. They pick up where the scripts leave off.
