# The Booking Pack

First paid product. Tests demand. Buyers become the founding intake. Due written and reviewed 30 November 2026, on sale 8 December 2026. Asana task 1220327362286117.

## Job

Kill two objections: they ghost me, and I don't know what to say in DMs. Everything inside already exists as an NYP template or process. The work is rewriting it for a stranger.

## Contents

1. DM-to-Booking scripts. Instagram DM replies that move a couple from first message to a booked call.
2. Enquiry-to-Booking sequence. The staged first reply (its only job is to book the call), the three-calendar date check, the competing enquiry paragraph for when two couples want the same date, the two day follow-up to-do.
3. Proposal template. A note from you, not a quote. First person singular, deliverables as dot points, exact gallery delivery dates.
4. Objection replies. Price pushback, the budget question, still deciding, partner wants someone else, fewer hours. Norm's position on discounting.
5. How to use this pack. One page: the order things fire in, and what to change for your own business.
6. See it done (added 9 Nov 2026). Screenshots of Norm's own Dubsado proposal, names and prices blurred, plus two recordings of Norm walking a couple through a proposal: a 15 minute audio chat and an hour long Google Meet consultation. Recording briefs in `media/recording-briefs.md`. The recordings show the proposal walk-through only. The call structure and questions stay in the Consult Call Kit.

## Source material (PC sessions)

Wedding enquiry workflow, ESE proposal email template, NYP DM scripts, objection replies, NYP offer changes. Strip venue names, client names, NYP pricing and anything that only makes sense inside NYP.
