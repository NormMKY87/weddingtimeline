## Items that need Norm, not a rewrite

1. **Album rule wording.** The brief says "never gift the album". The Vance offer architecture says the engagement session gift should convert to a print or album credit, not a discount. I have written the stance as: never cut the price, never give away a physical product to close, if you move anything move the scope or offer a credit toward something they would buy later. Confirm that is your position for strangers who may not sell albums at all.
2. **Gallery delivery dates.** The brief wants exact dates in the proposal. The NYP brain rule says no delivery number in copy. I have followed the brief for the product and noted the NYP rule as an aside the buyer can ignore. Confirm.
3. **Section 2 of the DM playbook** is written fresh from Quinn v6.3 and your sent DMs. Laura Higgins material is flagged and rewritten in a short table at the end of Part 1, continuing `laura-flags.md`.
