# Stranger review log

One pass through every part of the Booking Pack, read as a part-time wedding photographer in another city who has never heard of Norman Yap Photography. Done 8 November 2026 against the five markdown files in this folder. Every cut from the Dropbox source and every change made on the review pass is listed.

The test for each line: would it make sense, and would it still be true, for someone who shoots 8 weddings a year in Newcastle with a day job and no idea who Norm is.

## Result

All five parts read cleanly to a stranger. No venue names, client names, colleague names, prices, booking links or NYP tool names remain. Two voice slips were found on the review pass and fixed. One open decision was being stated as settled and has been softened.
