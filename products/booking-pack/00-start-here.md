# Start here

What this pack is for, and what you have when you finish it.

## The problem this fixes

Most photographers who want more bookings go looking for more leads. I did too. Then I counted. Enquiries were arriving. They were dying in my inbox and my DMs, somewhere between the first message and a reply, and again between my reply and a yes. The leads were never the problem. The gaps were.

This pack closes two gaps. The gap between a lead arriving and you responding. And the gap between your response and a booking. Tighten those two and your conversion goes up without a single extra enquiry. One more booking out of the leads you already get is worth more than chasing ten more leads you cannot convert. That is the bucket this pack fixes. Conversion, not volume.

## What it will help you do

- Reply to every DM and enquiry fast, in your own voice, with the one job of booking a short chat.
- Check your dates properly so you never take back a yes.
- Send a proposal the same day as the call that reads like a note from you.
- Answer the five objections that stop couples booking, without discounting.
- Build a follow-up habit so nobody falls through the gap again.

## What you have when you have done it

A system. Not a folder of templates, a sequence that fires in a known order, personalised to your business, where every message has one job and leads to the next step.

- **Personalised.** Every script has been rewritten in your words, with your reason for the call, your dates, your resource, your referral.
- **Intentional.** You know why each line is there. The callouts beside every DM screen tell you, so you can change the line and keep the reason.
- **Systematic.** The same thing happens every time an enquiry arrives, whether you are at your day job or on a shoot. The to-do is set, the follow-up is queued, the proposal has a date on it.
- **A cadence.** Reply, nudge, follow up, close the loop, on days you chose in advance, with the automated parts automated and the personal parts left to you.
- **Confidence.** You can present your value, hold your price, handle an objection and ask for the booking without feeling like a salesperson, because none of this is pushy.

That last point is the one I care about most. Sales, done this way, is recommending what you do and finding out whether you are a good fit for each other. If you are, you book the date. If you are not, you say so warmly and point them somewhere good. Both are a win. The only loss is the couple who never heard back.

## How to work through it

Anything in [square brackets] is yours to change. Lines marked PERSONAL are where you write one real sentence about the couple in front of you. Nothing here carries a price. The price of this pack is the only number I set, and the price of your weddings is yours alone.

Read Part 5 first. It is one page and it shows the order things fire in. Then go part by part, writing your own version as you go. Two evenings is enough for the scripts. The proposal takes a third. Part 6 is there to watch and listen to once you have your own version drafted, so you can hear how the words sound out loud.
