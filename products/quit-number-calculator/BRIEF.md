# Quit-Number Calculator

Free lead magnet and list builder. Due live 16 November 2026. Asana task 1220327478129069.

## Job

Answer the one question the buyer is stuck on: can I leave my job, and when. Collect an email in exchange for the result. Filter out hobbyists, who will not enter a salary.

## Inputs

- Current salary (gross annual, plus a toggle for take-home if they know it)
- Employer super contribution (default to the current guarantee rate, editable)
- Paid leave they would lose (weeks per year)
- Weddings shot this year and average sale per wedding
- Direct cost per wedding (second shooter, travel, editing, albums)
- Cash buffer wanted (months of household expenses)

## Outputs

- Quit number: the net annual income the business must produce to replace the job, after tax, super and leave are accounted for. Compare net to net, never gross wedding income to a net payslip.
- Gap: quit number minus current net wedding profit.
- Bookings required at the current average sale, and at two higher averages.
- Provisional quit date: when the buffer is funded at the current trajectory.
