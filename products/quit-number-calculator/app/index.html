A tool from Norman Yap, for photographers with a day job

Can I leave my job, and when?

I was a paramedic shooting weddings on the side. This is the maths I wish I had run before I handed in my notice. Eleven numbers, most of them already on your payslip, and it gives you the top end: the profit the business must make to replace everything the job pays, the gap, the bookings, and the dates. Plan for that and anything less is a bonus.

For wedding photographers who are already booking paid weddings, in any country. If you are not earning from weddings yet, come back when you are.
