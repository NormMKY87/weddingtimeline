A tool from Norman Yap, for photographers with a day job

Can I leave my job, and when?

I was a paramedic shooting weddings on the side. This is the maths I wish I had run before I handed in my notice. It compares your take-home pay to what the business must actually earn, builds in a buffer, and gives you a date. Twenty minutes, your real numbers, no guessing. It works in any country, because every rate comes from your own payslip and your own tax office.

For wedding photographers who are already booking paid weddings. If you are not earning from weddings yet, follow along and come back when you are.
