# The Warm List Emails (working title)

Product 08. Added to the side products list by Norm on 14 November 2026. Status: draft v0.1 written and designed in a cloud session, waiting on Norm's review. No Asana task yet.

## Job

Emails a photographer can send this week to people who already know their work, so a quiet month does not start with strangers.

## Contents

1. Start here. What a warm list is, the three rules behind every email, how to read each one.
2. Build your list. Six groups, where to find them, what to write down.
3. The map. All eleven emails, who each goes to and when.
4. Part one, past couples. Emails 1 to 7: the gallery follow-up, the album and print window, the anniversary note, the review ask, the introduction, the family session invitation, the newly engaged guest.
5. Part two, venues and vendors. Emails 8 to 11: the venue gallery, the vendor thank you, the open dates note, the open dates swap.
6. The sending plan. Two weeks, one group a day, then the routine after every wedding and a tally sheet.

Files: `00-start-here.md` to `03-sending-plan.md`. PDF: `the-warm-list-emails.pdf`, 20 pages, built with `python products/pdf-kit/build.py warm-list-emails`.

## What it does not cover

Never sell the same scripts twice. Checked on 14 November: the Booking Pack and the Follow-Up Vault hold no email to a past couple, a family, a venue or a vendor. They stop at the signed contract. This product starts after the wedding.

- Enquiry replies and the proposal stay in The Booking Pack.
- Follow-ups and ghosted lead revival stay in The Follow-Up Vault.
- The vendor intro sent after a consult stays in flagship lesson 4.3. Emails 8 and 9 here go out after a wedding, with photographs.
