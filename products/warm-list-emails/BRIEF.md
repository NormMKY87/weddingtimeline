# The Warm List Emails (working title)

Product 08. Proposed by Norm on 13 November 2026. Status: mockups built, nothing written, not yet on the side products list. No Asana task yet.

## Job

Emails a photographer can send this week to people who already know their work, so a quiet month does not start with strangers.

## Who it covers

Only people the photographer has already worked with or for:

1. Past couples (gallery follow-up, album and print reminder, anniversary note).
2. Families of past couples (family session invitation).
3. The venue, the planner and the vendors from a wedding already photographed (venue gallery, vendor thank you).

## Who it does not cover

The rule is never sell the same scripts twice, so these stay where they are:

- Enquiry replies and the proposal: The Booking Pack.
- Follow-ups and ghosted lead revival: The Follow-Up Vault.
- The vendor intro sent after a consult: flagship lesson 4.3.

## Structure (proposed)

One page per email. Each page carries who it is for, when to send it, the one line to change, the email with [placeholders], why it is written that way, and when to skip it. A one page map at the front lists every email by group.

## Source rule

Every email is one Norm has sent himself, rewritten for a stranger with names and venues removed. The source is his own sent mail and Dubsado canned emails.

The KT Merry Insiders material in Dropbox (`/18_Education & Professional Development/KT Merry Insiders`) is a paid course Norm bought to learn from. It stays private study notes. None of its wording, template set, order or titles goes into this product, reworded or not. A topic that also appears there is fine to teach when Norm has done it himself and tells it with his own example. This matches the rule already in force for Laura Higgins and Alex Hormozi material.

The inside pages in the mockups (`mockups/screens/draft-pages.html`) are placeholders written on 13 November without opening the KT Merry PDFs. They show the page system. They are not the product.

## Needed from Norm (PC session)

- The emails he has sent to past couples, families and vendors that got a reply or a booking. Sent mail, Dubsado canned emails, WhatsApp.
- For each one: who it went to, when, and what happened.
- Any he tried that did not work. Those earn a "when to skip it" line.
