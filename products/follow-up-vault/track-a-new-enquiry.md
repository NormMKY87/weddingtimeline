# Track A: new enquiry, no reply

You sent the first reply (Booking Pack). Nothing came back. These five messages run from day 2 to day 21. Messages A6 and A7 (revival and close the file) are in `revival-and-close.md`.

How to read each message:

- Anything in [square brackets] is yours to fill. Delete the brackets.
- Lines marked PERSONAL are where you write one real sentence about this couple. Never leave a generic line there.
- Every message has an email version. Use the SMS, Instagram DM or WhatsApp version alongside it, or instead of it, where that is how the couple came to you.
- Keep every email in the same thread as your first reply. Hit reply, keep the subject.
- Sign off with your first name. Your normal email signature does the rest.

## A1. The detail and the question

Day 2. Fully personalised. Email in the thread, plus a short SMS or DM.

This is the message that gets most of the replies on this track, and it only works because it proves you read their enquiry. Open their form. Find the one thing they wrote that was not a box they had to fill. That is your detail.

**Email**

Subject: Re: [your first reply subject]

> Hi [Partner 1] and [Partner 2],
>
> A quick one, because I kept thinking about your enquiry after I replied.
>
> PERSONAL: [One real sentence about something they told you. Their venue, how they met, the thing they said they cared about most. Example: "A ceremony on the lawn at four in the afternoon in March is my favourite light of the year."]
>
> PERSONAL: [One easy question that follows from that sentence. Example: "Is the ceremony outside too, or just the photos after?"]
>
> Your date is still open with me. No rush on the reply.
>
> [Your first name]

**SMS**

> Hi [Partner 1], [Your first name] here. I sent a note about your [Month] wedding on [Day], with one question about [the detail]. No rush, it's in your inbox whenever you're ready.

**Instagram DM** (use instead of the email if they enquired through Instagram)

> Hi [Partner 1]! Thanks again for reaching out about [Month]. I've been thinking about [the detail] since. [The question]? Your date is still open with me, and there's no rush.

**WhatsApp**

> Hi [Partner 1], it's [Your first name]. I've been thinking about your [Month] wedding since you wrote. [The question]? The date's still open with me. No rush at all.

Skip it when: they have opened your booking link, replied anywhere, or booked a call. Any of those and this track is over. You are in a conversation now.
