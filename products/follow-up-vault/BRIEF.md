# The Follow-Up Vault

Order bump at Booking Pack checkout. Due 7 December 2026. Asana task 1220327478129133.

## Job

Every follow-up message between first reply and signed contract, by day and channel, so nobody has to invent a nudge at 9pm after a shift.

## Structure (Norm, 8 Nov 2026)

Two tracks: new enquiry with no reply, and proposal sent with no decision. Six to seven messages per track to start, with room to grow. The first follow-up is fully personalised, the second half personalised, the rest are templates with one variable. Full day-by-day map in `sessions/03-follow-up-vault.md`.

## Contents

1. The cadence map. Both tracks day by day, with the channel for each touch (email, SMS, DM, WhatsApp).
2. The messages. One ready-to-send message per touchpoint, with the variable to change marked and a line on when to skip it.
3. Ghosted lead revival. The sequence for a lead that went quiet, including the last message that closes the file politely.
4. The to-do rule. How to set the follow-up to-do the moment you reply, in any CRM or a calendar.

## Source material (PC sessions)

NYP follow-up cadence, follow-up to-do rules, Dubsado automated to-dos, lead follow-up tracker.

## Done when

The cadence map and every message are complete, designed as a short PDF, and read cleanly to a stranger.
