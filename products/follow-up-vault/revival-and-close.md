# Ghosted lead revival and closing the file

Four messages. Two per track. The revival message brings a quiet lead back with something true. The close-the-file message ends the sequence kindly, releases the date, and leaves the door open.

## The rules for revival

- Point to something true. A wedding you shot last weekend at a venue like theirs. A resource you just finished. A date near theirs that just booked. If none of it is true, do not send it. Wait until something is, up to ten days past the mapped day.
- Never manufacture scarcity. "Dates are filling fast" is a lie unless you can name the dates. If your calendar is open, say nothing about your calendar.
- Never count the unanswered messages. "I've emailed four times" is a guilt trip, and it does not work.
- Never ask "are you still interested". It invites a no and sounds like everyone else.
- One revival per track. If it does not land, you close the file.

## The rules for closing the file

- Always send it. The close-the-file message is the one touch that never gets skipped. It ends every unanswered enquiry on your terms and gives the couple an easy out.
- Release the date the same day, in your calendar and your CRM. Lost reason: no response.
- Offer to keep in touch only if they say yes. Reply "yes please" is the only way onto your nurture list.
- Then stop. A couple who comes back after a close-the-file message does so because you stopped, not despite it.

## A7. Close the file, Track A

Day 60. Template, one variable: [Wedding date]. Email only.

**Email**

Subject: Closing the file on [Wedding date]

> Hi [Partner 1] and [Partner 2],
>
> This is my last note about [Wedding date]. I'll take it as a no, release the date, and stop filling your inbox.
>
> If I've got that wrong, one word back fixes it.
>
> Whoever is photographing your day, I hope it's wonderful. And if you'd like the odd useful thing for the planning from me, reply "yes please". Otherwise, this is goodbye and good luck from me.
>
> [Your first name]

**Date-gone variant** (send early, any day, when another couple books their date)

Subject: [Wedding date] has been booked

> Hi [Partner 1] and [Partner 2],
>
> Another couple has booked [Wedding date], so I wanted to tell you rather than leave you wondering.
>
> If your plans change, or you have a second date in mind, I'd love to hear from you. And if you've already booked someone, I hope the day is everything you want it to be.
>
> [Your first name]

Skip it when: never. This message ends every unanswered enquiry.

## After the file is closed

1. Mark the enquiry lost in your CRM the same day. Reason: no response. If the date went to another couple, reason: date taken.
2. Release the date in every calendar you check availability against.
3. Delete every remaining to-do for this couple.
4. If they replied "yes please" to the nurture line, add them to your keep-in-touch list. Nobody else goes on it.
5. If they come back later, they start a fresh conversation, not a sequence. Reply like a person, book the call, and let the Booking Pack take it from there.
